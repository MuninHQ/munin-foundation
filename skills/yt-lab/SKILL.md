# YT-LAB Skill

Use this skill when the user asks to build, run, review, prioritize, or analyze the faceless YouTube experiment.

## Required context

Before execution, establish or retrieve:

- active channel hypothesis;
- primary niche;
- audience promise;
- current stage;
- published video count;
- remaining budget from the R$500 cap;
- available time within the 5-hour weekly cap;
- latest comparable analytics.

Do not invent missing analytics. Mark them unavailable.

## Topic evaluation

Score each candidate from 0 to 5:

- evergreen durability;
- demand signal;
- series depth;
- differentiation;
- evidence availability;
- advertiser value;
- copyright/reused-content risk;
- production effort;
- factual/reputational risk.

Reject a candidate when copyright or factual risk is 4 or 5.

Return both the weighted score and the reason for the decision.

## Evidence pack standard

Every approved topic needs:

- research question;
- audience promise;
- primary and reputable secondary sources;
- verified claims;
- uncertain or disputed claims;
- dates and numbers requiring revalidation;
- quotations with attribution;
- original narrative angle;
- visual sourcing notes;
- claims that must not be made.

## Script standard

A script must contain:

- a hook that accurately represents the video;
- an explicit viewer payoff;
- logical sections with narrative progression;
- source-backed factual claims;
- original synthesis rather than stitched summaries;
- visual guidance that avoids dependency on copyrighted clips;
- closing payoff and optional next-video bridge.

Do not pad duration with repetition.

## Packaging standard

For each video, prepare:

- 3 title hypotheses;
- 2 thumbnail concepts;
- the curiosity mechanism for each;
- the claim the packaging makes;
- the risk of misleading the viewer;
- the expected audience segment.

Packaging must not promise information absent from the video.

## Production plan

Prefer a low-cost, replaceable stack. Separate:

- narration;
- original/generative visuals;
- licensed or public-domain assets;
- diagrams and motion templates;
- captions;
- music and sound effects;
- final edit.

Record the commercial-use basis for every external asset category.

## Publication readiness

Return `BLOCKED` unless all are true:

- evidence pack complete;
- script fact-checked;
- originality check passed;
- external asset licenses documented;
- no unapproved voice cloning;
- title/thumbnail approved;
- final render reviewed by the user;
- source description reviewed;
- publication explicitly approved.

## Analytics review

Compare videos at comparable ages. Review:

- impressions;
- click-through rate;
- first 30-second retention;
- average percentage viewed;
- average view duration;
- returning viewers;
- subscriber conversion;
- production time;
- cost;
- performance by topic cluster.

Classify the main bottleneck as one of:

- topic selection;
- packaging;
- opening/hook;
- narrative/retention;
- production quality;
- audience mismatch;
- insufficient data.

Recommend one primary test for the next video rather than changing everything simultaneously.

## Experiment decisions

Use one of:

- CONTINUE — current hypothesis still plausible;
- ITERATE — retain the niche and test a specific correction;
- PIVOT — change topic cluster, format, or audience promise;
- PAUSE — capacity, cost, or governance issue;
- STOP — evidence no longer justifies further investment.

Always show the evidence supporting the decision and the next human approval required.
