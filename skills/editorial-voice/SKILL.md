# Editorial Voice — Munin Adaptation

Use this skill for prose published or sent in André's name. Read the relevant recent examples, personal brand context, thesis inventory, publication history, and channel constraints before drafting. A current user instruction overrides inferred voice patterns.

## Preserve substance and voice

Keep every factual claim that matters and never invent names, figures, dates, quotations, personal experiences, sources, or certainty. Match André's established sentence rhythm, vocabulary, directness, technical altitude, and normal degree of formality. Preserve deliberate quirks when they are consistent across examples; do not replace his voice with generic creator language.

Prefer a concrete consequence, tension, decision, or observation over a manufactured hook. Use first person only for experiences or views André actually owns. Explain technical subjects in plain language without flattening the underlying governance, product, infrastructure, or market consequence.

## Remove synthetic writing patterns

Revise inflated significance, vague expert attribution, unsupported consensus, sales language, abstract filler, forced groups of three, repetitive sentence openings, excessive headings, decorative bold, emoji decoration, stock motivational closings, and chatbot residue. Replace grand claims with precise claims. Name a source when available; otherwise narrow or remove the assertion.

Do not apply mechanical bans. Punctuation, paragraph length, and informality should follow André's samples and the target channel. Natural writing may have uneven rhythm, short paragraphs, qualified opinions, and direct transitions. Humanization must not reduce accuracy, evidence, nuance, or professional credibility.

## LinkedIn workflow

Check the proposed angle against publication history before drafting. Reject or materially differentiate repeated theses, metaphors, examples, and opening structures. Build the post around one central argument, connect it to a credible source or owned experience when available, and close with a useful implication or genuine question rather than engagement bait.

Run Munin's Brand Preflight and evidence checks before approval or scheduling. Follow the current LinkedIn image policy: do not add AJ, logos, monograms, signatures, watermarks, or personal visual branding unless André explicitly asks to reintroduce them. This rule overrides older brand documentation that says otherwise.

External publication remains governed by the Publisher lifecycle. This skill may draft and revise locally; it never bypasses approval, scheduling, deduplication, or connector controls.

## Final check

Read the text aloud once. Confirm that each paragraph advances the argument, every factual claim is supported, the opening is specific, the conclusion earns its place, and the result sounds like André rather than a content template.
