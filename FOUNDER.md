# Founder Note

Munin began in 2026 with a simple desire: build a personal assistant that could remember context and become genuinely useful over time.

The idea evolved into a larger question:

> What should the relationship between a human being and computation look like after AI becomes ubiquitous?

This repository exists to preserve the reasoning, evidence, rejected ideas, decisions, and experiments behind that question.

The project must remain ambitious without becoming detached from reality. We will imagine boldly, validate honestly, and change direction without ego when evidence demands it.
