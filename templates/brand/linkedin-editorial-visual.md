# LinkedIn Editorial Visual Template

## Objective

Create consistent LinkedIn visuals for André Jardim that reinforce an executive identity at the intersection of product, financial innovation, regulation, AI and digital infrastructure.

## Canonical composition

- Portrait or square format suitable for LinkedIn.
- Near-black minimalist background.
- One central symbolic object.
- Premium corporate-magazine aesthetic.
- Subtle blue accents and controlled warm metallic highlights.
- AJ Rune monogram integrated discreetly into the central object.
- No body copy, hashtags, URLs or captions inside the artwork.

## Brand anchor

Use the proprietary AJ monogram defined in `docs/brand/aj-rune-brand-system.md`.

The monogram must:

- preserve the merged A/J construction;
- feel engraved, embossed or structurally integrated;
- remain subtle enough not to overpower the post theme;
- use silver, graphite, bronze or restrained gold according to the scene;
- avoid generic typed initials.

## Prompt template

```text
Create a premium editorial LinkedIn visual for André Jardim about [TOPIC].

Scene: [CENTRAL SYMBOLIC OBJECT] placed at the center of a dark, minimalist composition. The object should communicate [CORE IDEA] through form, material and lighting rather than explanatory text.

Visual identity: corporate magazine cover, cinematic low-key lighting, charcoal-black background, brushed metal or translucent glass, subtle electric-blue accents, restrained warm highlights, high-end 3D realism, executive and timeless.

Brand integration: incorporate the proprietary André Jardim AJ Rune monogram discreetly into the central object as an engraved, embossed or illuminated detail. The monogram is a merged A/J Nordic-inspired symbol, not plain typed letters.

Composition rules:
- one dominant symbolic object;
- clean negative space;
- no repeated icons;
- no text, captions, hashtags or URL;
- no excessive cyberpunk clutter;
- no generic stock-photo aesthetic;
- no people unless explicitly required;
- preserve visual coherence with the AJ Rune / Munin brand system.

Output: [ASPECT RATIO / SIZE].
```

## Drex / financial infrastructure variant

```text
Create a premium editorial LinkedIn visual about the evolution of Drex, tokenization, regulation and financial products.

Show a single translucent metallic digital token hovering over a discreet holographic platform in a dark financial-network environment. Surround it with a limited set of distinct, subtle nodes representing banking, security, identity, AI, tokenization, users and analytics. Do not repeat icons.

Replace any generic currency symbol in the center of the token with the proprietary André Jardim AJ Rune monogram. The monogram must look engraved into the token and use restrained warm bronze-gold illumination, while the environment keeps subtle blue accents.

Maintain a premium corporate editorial style, strong negative space and cinematic lighting. No text, no URL, no hashtags and no captions inside the image.
```

## Quality checklist

Before approval, confirm:

- [ ] The AJ Rune monogram is the correct proprietary shape.
- [ ] There is only one central focal object.
- [ ] No icons are duplicated accidentally.
- [ ] The image contains no body text.
- [ ] Blue accents are subtle.
- [ ] The visual feels executive, not gaming-oriented.
- [ ] The theme is understandable without becoming literal or crowded.
- [ ] The output is suitable for LinkedIn crop and mobile viewing.
