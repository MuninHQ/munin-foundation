# Research with Evidence

1. Define the exact decision the research must support.
2. Prefer official documentation, primary sources, standards, repositories, and original data.
3. Record source date and check whether the information may have changed.
4. Distinguish verified facts, source claims, assumptions, and inference.
5. Surface contradictions and missing evidence rather than smoothing them over.
6. Summarize only what materially affects the decision.
7. End with recommendation, confidence, limitations, and the next evidence-gathering step.

Never fabricate citations, quotes, benchmarks, or validation.
