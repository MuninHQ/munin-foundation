# Munin Engineering Constitution

- **Version:** 0.1
- **Status:** Draft
- **Date:** 2026-07-13

## Article I — Ownership

A person's state belongs to that person.

## Article II — Evidence

Every durable assertion must be traceable to evidence or explicit user input.

## Article III — Uncertainty

Inference must never be displayed or stored as confirmed fact.

## Article IV — Control

Users can inspect, correct, export, and delete their state.

## Article V — Consent

Collection, retention, inference, and action are constrained by explicit purpose and consent.

## Article VI — Provider independence

No model provider may become indispensable to the domain architecture.

## Article VII — Explainability

Every state transition must provide an operational explanation.

## Article VIII — Reversibility

Automation defaults to reversible behavior. High-risk or irreversible actions require explicit confirmation.

## Article IX — Minimal context

External reasoning providers receive the smallest sufficient context for the task.

## Article X — Simplicity

Complexity must be earned by evidence.
