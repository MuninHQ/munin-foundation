# Content Engine

> Shared capability for turning research, project work, professional experience, and market analysis into consistent executive content.

## Purpose

The Content Engine converts knowledge produced across the portfolio into professional authority, strategic conversations, and career opportunities.

It is a shared capability used by:

- Career Operations
- North Star OS
- Munin Foundation
- André Intelligence Platform (AIP)
- Intellectual Production
- YT-LAB, when a topic is suitable for audiovisual reuse

The Content Engine is not a separate product or platform. It is an operating capability governed by North Star and executed through a common editorial workflow.

## Outcomes

1. Maintain a consistent and credible professional presence.
2. Turn large research assets into multiple smaller publications.
3. Connect original thinking with active career objectives.
4. Reduce the effort required to move from idea to publication.
5. Preserve sources, decisions, drafts, and reusable assets in a versioned system.

## Editorial pipeline

`Idea → Research → Draft → Review → Approved → Scheduled → Published → Repurposed`

### Stage definitions

| Stage | Exit criterion |
|---|---|
| Idea | Topic, audience, intended outcome, and source project are recorded |
| Research | Claims, evidence, references, and original point of view are sufficient |
| Draft | A complete channel-specific artifact exists |
| Review | Accuracy, clarity, tone, confidentiality, and strategic alignment are checked |
| Approved | André has approved the final publication version |
| Scheduled | Channel and publication date are defined |
| Published | Final public artifact or publication record is captured |
| Repurposed | Reusable derivatives are created or intentionally declined |

## Ownership

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| André | Editorial owner, final approval, publication, and response to strategic conversations |
| ChatGPT | Editorial strategy, positioning, calendar, idea development, synthesis, drafting support, and executive review |
| Claude | Repository artifacts, structured drafts, templates, transformations, automation, technical documentation, and version maintenance |

No content may be published automatically without André's explicit approval.

## Editorial guardrails

1. Career relevance and intellectual credibility take precedence over volume.
2. Every publication must contain a clear point of view, useful insight, or original synthesis.
3. Confidential, personal, client, employer, and commercially sensitive information must not be exposed.
4. Claims that depend on current facts require source verification.
5. AI assistance must improve quality without fabricating experience, evidence, quotations, or results.
6. Munin and AIP content must distinguish hypotheses, experiments, accepted decisions, and delivered capabilities.
7. One strong asset should be repurposed before unrelated content is created from scratch.
8. Vanity metrics do not override relevance to target roles, networks, and strategic positioning.

## Default content brief

Each item should record:

- Working title
- Source project
- Strategic objective
- Target audience
- Core thesis
- Supporting evidence
- Channel and format
- Call to action, when appropriate
- Confidentiality review
- Owner
- Current pipeline stage
- Target publication date
- Published URL or publication evidence
- Repurposing opportunities

- Maintain a prioritized idea backlog.
- Select one flagship theme at a time.
- Publish at a sustainable cadence rather than maximizing frequency.
- Review performance and strategic conversations in the portfolio SITREP.
- Reuse each flagship asset across suitable formats before starting the next large asset.

## Metrics

### Production

- Approved publications per month
- Lead time from idea to approval
- Percentage of flagship assets repurposed
- Reuse ratio per research asset

### Reach and relevance

- Impressions and qualified engagement
- Saves, substantive comments, and direct shares
- New relevant connections
- Conversations with recruiters, hiring leaders, founders, executives, and domain experts

### Strategic outcomes

- Interviews or opportunities influenced by content
- Invitations to collaborate, speak, advise, or contribute
- Evidence that target audiences associate André with the intended domains

Metrics should be interpreted qualitatively and over time. A low-volume publication that creates a high-value conversation may outperform a high-reach generic post.

## Definition of ready

The capability is operational when:

- The workflow and templates are stored in the repository.
- A prioritized content backlog exists.
- The Drex package completes the full pipeline.
- Owners and approval rules are consistently followed.
- Content performance is included in recurring SITREP reviews.
