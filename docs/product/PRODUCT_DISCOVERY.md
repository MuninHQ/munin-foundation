# Product Discovery

## Initial segment

Professionals balancing career transition, family, learning, projects, and multiple digital tools.

## Candidate jobs to be done

- Continue an important workflow without rebuilding context.
- Prepare a decision using relevant information from several life areas.
- Turn new information into updated projects and next actions.
- Surface what deserves attention today.
- Preserve continuity when changing devices or AI providers.

## Required outputs

- Interview notes
- Problem ranking
- Current-solution map
- Evidence log
- Prototype results
- Revised category thesis
- MVP recommendation
