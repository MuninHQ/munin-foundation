# Why Munin Exists

Digital life is fragmented across communication, calendars, files, applications, and temporary AI conversations.

People repeatedly reconstruct context. Existing tools retain isolated information but rarely understand why it matters together. Munin explores a model in which the person—not the application—is the center.

## Foundational hypothesis

A continuous, portable, explainable, and user-owned representation of relevant personal context can create more durable value than isolated AI conversations.

Our responsibility is to test this hypothesis honestly.
