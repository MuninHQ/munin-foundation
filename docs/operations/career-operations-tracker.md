# Career Operations Tracker

> Canonical operating source for P0 Career Operations.
>
> Last updated: **2026-07-27**

## Objective

Convert high-fit opportunities into applications, strategic conversations, interviews, offers, and informed decisions without allowing internal system-building to displace market execution.

## Canonical pipeline

`Discovery → Fit Score → Investigation → Application → Outreach → Interview → Offer → Decision`

## Operating rules

1. Pursue only roles with credible strategic fit.
2. Record one explicit next action and due date for every active opportunity.
3. Use the Platinum CV as the canonical master; create only short, targeted variants.
4. Follow up on time and record the outcome.
5. Do not commit confidential personal, compensation, contact, or employer information to this public repository.
6. Store sensitive opportunity details in the approved private career workspace and use anonymized IDs here when necessary.

### Fit score guide

| Dimension | Weight | Questions |
|---|---:|---|
| Role and seniority | 25% | Does the mandate match senior or executive scope? |
| Domain alignment | 20% | Payments, Open Finance, financial infrastructure, digital assets, identity, innovation, or applied AI? |
| Evidence of impact | 20% | Can André demonstrate relevant outcomes and cases? |
| Strategic attractiveness | 15% | Does the opportunity improve trajectory, learning, network, or market position? |
| Practical viability | 10% | Location, language, work model, and compensation direction are viable? |
| Access and timing | 10% | Is there a realistic application, referral, or conversation path now? |

**Default threshold:** prioritize opportunities with a fit score of **80% or higher**. Exceptions require an explicit rationale.

## Daily review

1. What is the single most valuable career outcome for today?
2. Which opportunity has the nearest material deadline?
3. Which follow-up is due or overdue?
4. Which external conversation should be initiated?
5. Is internal building displacing applications, outreach, or interview preparation?

## Weekly operating review

1. Recalculate active fit scores when new information appears.
2. Remove low-fit or stale opportunities.
3. Review funnel conversion and aging by stage.
4. Verify that every active item has a next action and date.
5. Review networking and follow-up consistency.
6. Prepare upcoming interviews.
7. Select the next seven career actions.

## Stage exit criteria

| Stage | Exit criterion |
|---|---|
| Discovery | Opportunity is captured with source and basic role information |
| Fit Score | Weighted fit is recorded with a pursue / hold / reject decision |
| Investigation | Company, mandate, decision-makers, risks, and application route are understood |
| Application | Approved application package is submitted and evidence is recorded |
| Outreach | Relevant contact is identified and an approved message is sent |
| Interview | Preparation package exists and each conversation outcome is recorded |
| Offer | Terms, strategic fit, risks, and alternatives are assessed privately |
| Decision | Accept, decline, pause, or close decision is recorded with rationale |

## Source-of-truth boundary

- This public repository stores the operating model, anonymized status, templates, and aggregate metrics.
- The private career workspace stores names, personal contacts, compensation data, CV variants, messages, interview notes, and application evidence.
- The Executive Dashboard summarizes portfolio-level status and decisions.

## Definition of operational

The tracker is operational when:

- At least five active opportunities are recorded in the private source of truth.
- Every active opportunity has a stage, fit score, next action, and due date.
- Follow-ups are reviewed at least twice per week.
- Weekly KPIs have a current baseline.
- One weekly Career Operations review has been completed and recorded.
