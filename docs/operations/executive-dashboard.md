## 10. Daily SITREP

### Today

- **Primary outcome:** Advance the single most important P0 deliverable.
- **Secondary outcome:** Complete at most one enabling P1 or P2 deliverable.
- **Avoid:** Unplanned architecture expansion, low-fit vacancies, and content polishing without an approval threshold.

### Daily questions

1. What materially changed since the previous update?
2. What is the most important result to produce today?
3. What is blocked?
4. What decision is required?
5. What can be stopped, deferred, or simplified?

## 12. Command interface

### `SITREP`

Report current status, material changes, completed work, blockers, required decisions, and next milestones.

### `INCREP`

Report a new event, opportunity, risk, dependency, or material change and assess its effect on priorities.

### `EXECUTE`

Perform the highest-priority accepted action that is sufficiently specified and safe to execute.

### Execution rule

When `EXECUTE` is issued:

1. Prefer P0 work.
2. Use accepted specifications and decisions.
3. Do not invent authorization for external publication, communication, financial action, or irreversible changes.
4. Record the result, commit, artifact, or blocker.
5. Update the dashboard when the portfolio state materially changes.
