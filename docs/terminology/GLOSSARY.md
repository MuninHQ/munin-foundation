# Glossary

## Munin
The company and research initiative.

## Human Computing
A provisional category hypothesis for computing organized around a persistent, user-controlled model of the person.

## Continuity
Preservation of relevant context, commitments, history, and meaning across time, tools, devices, and intelligence providers.

## Human State
A provisional representation of the person's currently relevant circumstances.

## Life State Engine
A proposed system for deriving, updating, querying, and explaining Human State.

## Event
A time-bound occurrence that may update state.

## Mission
A high-priority direction that influences planning and recommendations.

## Council
A reasoning pattern that considers several relevant perspectives before synthesis.

## RFC
A proposal for a durable conceptual, product, technical, or governance change.

## ADR
A record of an accepted decision and its consequences.
