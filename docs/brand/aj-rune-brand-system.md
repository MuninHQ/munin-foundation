# AJ Rune — Brand System

## Purpose

A personal identity system for André Jardim that connects executive leadership, financial innovation, product strategy, AI, digital assets and the Munin ecosystem through a minimalist Nordic-inspired visual language.

## Core principles

- Premium editorial aesthetic.
- Dark, minimalist backgrounds.
- A single strong symbolic object.
- Metallic silver, graphite and controlled blue accents.
- Nordic influence without copying historical runes literally.
- Executive, timeless and understated rather than fantasy or gaming-oriented.

## Primary mark — AJ Rune

The main mark merges the initials **A** and **J** into a proprietary monogram.

- **A / mountain / spear:** direction, ambition, ascent and purpose.
- **J / curved tail:** movement, continuity and connection.
- **Incomplete circle:** an evolving journey; systems and knowledge are always under construction.
- **Top branch/rune:** growth, vision and continuous evolution.
- **Side runic markers:** balance, protection and connection with what is essential.
- **Raven:** Munin, memory, knowledge and consciousness.
- **Central diamond:** focus, clarity and intention.

## Visual language

### Colors

- Background: near-black / charcoal.
- Primary metal: graphite, brushed steel or muted silver.
- Accent: restrained electric or editorial blue.
- Optional highlight: warm bronze or gold only for focal emphasis.

### Lighting

- Low-key cinematic lighting.
- Controlled reflections.
- Subtle depth and embossing.
- Avoid excessive glow, cyberpunk clutter or gaming aesthetics.

### Typography

- Elegant, high-legibility serif or refined sans-serif for the name.
- Wide letter spacing for institutional applications.
- Minimal supporting text.
- Never place long-form post copy inside the image.

## LinkedIn identity rules

- Use a dark premium editorial composition.
- Keep one central symbolic object.
- Use the AJ monogram subtly, not as oversized self-promotion.
- Prefer symbolic storytelling over literal diagrams.
- No body text inside the image.
- Maintain visual consistency across posts.
- Use blue accents sparingly.
- Avoid repeating identical icons in the same composition.

## Canonical tagline

**Liderança • Inovação • Propósito**

## Governance

Any new visual asset should preserve:

1. the proprietary AJ monogram;
2. the dark editorial base;
3. restrained metallic treatment;
4. subtle Nordic references;
5. the connection to Munin, memory and knowledge;
6. executive credibility over decorative complexity.
