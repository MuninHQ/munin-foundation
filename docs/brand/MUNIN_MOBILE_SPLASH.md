# Munin Mobile splash

The mobile opening screen uses the approved cinematic Nordic art as the visual source of truth.

- Primary symbol: raven.
- No arbitrary R-rune branding.
- The approved artwork is rendered directly rather than reconstructed with CSS geometry.
- Functional controls are transparent accessibility/touch layers over the artwork.
- Operational content remains separate and appears only after entry.
- Motion is limited to subtle whole-scene parallax and entry fade; reduced-motion preferences are respected.
