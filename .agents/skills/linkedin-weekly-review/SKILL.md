# LinkedIn Weekly Review

Use an explicitly authorized analytics connector/export. If no analytics source is accessible, report the missing evidence instead of inventing metrics.

Analyze post-level impressions/reach, reactions, comments, shares, clicks and engagement where available. Compare themes, formats and posting times.

Return what worked, what underperformed, likely explanations clearly labeled as inference, concrete adjustments for the following week, and three proposed topics aligned to the private professional context.

Do not publish or schedule posts automatically. Keep account identifiers and raw analytics outside Git.
