# YT-LAB — Primary Niche Decision v1

Status: **SELECTED**
Date: 2026-07-30
Market: Brazil / Portuguese
Format: faceless, narrated, long-form first

## Selected niche

**A infraestrutura invisível do dinheiro**

A documentary-explainer channel about the systems, institutions, technologies and historical decisions that make money move: Pix, cards, bank transfers, SWIFT, Open Finance, digital identity, stablecoins, central banks, fraud prevention and payment infrastructure.

## Positioning

> Explicar, de forma visual e acessível, o que acontece por trás das operações financeiras que usamos todos os dias.

The channel is educational, not financial advice. It does not recommend investments, products or institutions.

## Rejected alternatives

### General curiosities
Rejected as primary niche because differentiation is weak and production tends to become template-driven.

### Broad history
Retained as a secondary storytelling layer, but too broad for the first validation cycle.

### AI news
Rejected because it is not evergreen and would create a high-frequency research burden inconsistent with the five-hour weekly cap.

### Personal finance and investments
Rejected because it creates higher factual, regulatory and trust risk and depends more heavily on current recommendations.

## Editorial pillars

1. **Por trás de um pagamento** — the journey of a Pix, card purchase, boleto or international transfer.
2. **Sistemas que ninguém vê** — SPI, DICT, RSFN, clearing, settlement, messaging and identity.
3. **A história da infraestrutura financeira** — how past systems shaped today’s money movement.
4. **Tecnologias que podem mudar o dinheiro** — Open Finance, tokenization, stablecoins and digital identity, without hype.
5. **Falhas, golpes e defenses** — how systems detect risk and what happens when transactions fail.

## Audience hypothesis

Primary audience:
- Brazilian adults aged 25–44;
- interested in technology, finance, business and practical explanations;
- curious about how everyday systems work;
- not necessarily finance professionals.

Secondary audience:
- banking and fintech professionals;
- students of economics, technology, business and public policy.

## Format hypothesis

- Duration: 7–11 minutes.
- Narration: synthetic or licensed voice, natural cadence, no celebrity imitation.
- Structure: cold open → question → system map → step-by-step journey → why it matters → concise closing.
- Visuals: original diagrams first; licensed/public-domain assets second; generic stock only when it advances the explanation.
- Publication: one video every 7–10 days during the experiment.

## Governance gates

A video cannot advance unless it has:

- at least two reliable sources, including one primary source for system claims;
- a claim ledger linking factual claims to sources;
- an originality note explaining the unique analysis or visual model;
- asset provenance for every third-party visual/audio item;
- no investment recommendation or personalized financial advice;
- human approval before publication.

## Platform-policy implications

YouTube monetization policy requires original and authentic value and can reject repetitive, mass-produced or minimally transformed content. Therefore YT-LAB prohibits generic slideshow production and requires original narrative, diagrams and analysis in every episode.

Realistic AI-generated or meaningfully altered scenes must be disclosed during upload. AI used only for outlines, scripts, thumbnails, captions or non-realistic production assistance generally does not require disclosure, but the publication checklist must evaluate each asset.
