# PROJECT YT-LAB

## Mission

Validate, with minimal cost and bounded weekly effort, whether an AI-assisted faceless YouTube channel can reach repeatable audience growth and monetization without depending on André's image, voice, or personal brand.

YT-LAB is an experiment, not a promise of fast income.

## Hard constraints

- No personal image.
- No use of André's own voice.
- No dependency on a personal brand.
- Initial budget cap: R$500.
- Weekly operating limit: 5 hours.
- Prefer free or low-cost tools.
- Avoid copyright-sensitive formats and reused-content patterns.
- Human approval remains mandatory before publication.

## Current hypothesis

A faceless channel can become viable when it combines:

1. an evergreen niche with durable topic depth;
2. original research and synthesis;
3. repeatable production templates;
4. strong packaging: title, thumbnail, and first 30 seconds;
5. disciplined measurement and fast iteration.

## Candidate niche families

The experiment may evaluate:

- history and forgotten events;
- curiosities and explainers;
- technology and financial infrastructure;
- mysteries with verifiable sources;
- business and economic case studies.

A niche is not approved merely because it has high views. It must pass copyright, sustainability, cost, depth, and differentiation gates.

## Experiment stages

### Stage 0 — Setup

- Define channel hypothesis.
- Select one niche and one audience promise.
- Build topic scoring model.
- Create production templates.
- Define analytics baseline.

### Stage 1 — Pilot

- Produce 3 pilot videos.
- Publish at a sustainable cadence.
- Record production time and cost.
- Test at least two packaging approaches.

### Stage 2 — Validation

- Reach 10 published videos.
- Identify repeatable topic clusters.
- Compare retention, click-through rate, watch time, and subscriber conversion.
- Stop formats that repeatedly miss thresholds.

### Stage 3 — Scale or stop

Scale only when the channel demonstrates:

- repeatable production under the 5-hour weekly limit;
- acceptable copyright and reused-content risk;
- at least one topic cluster with improving performance;
- evidence that additional videos compound rather than reset progress.

Otherwise, document the learning and stop or pivot.

## Core workflow

```text
Research backlog
      ↓
Topic scoring
      ↓
Evidence pack
      ↓
Script
      ↓
Fact/copyright review
      ↓
Voice + visual assembly
      ↓
Packaging
      ↓
Human approval
      ↓
Publish
      ↓
Analytics review
      ↓
Learn / iterate / stop
```

## Definition of done for YT-LAB v1

YT-LAB v1 is ready when:

- the project charter exists;
- roles and approval gates are explicit;
- topic scoring and experiment metrics are defined;
- a low-cost production stack is documented;
- the first 10-video experiment can be executed without architectural decisions during production;
- every published video has an evidence pack, originality note, and post-publication review.

## Governance

The `youtube-lab-orchestrator` coordinates the workflow. It may prepare research, scripts, prompts, metadata, and analysis. It may not publish, purchase tools, accept licenses, or use copyrighted material without explicit human approval.
