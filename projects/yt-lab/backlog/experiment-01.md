# YT-LAB — Experiment 01 Backlog

Primary niche: **A infraestrutura invisível do dinheiro**
Experiment size: 10 long-form videos
Target cadence: one video every 7–10 days

## Scoring

Each topic is scored from 0–10 on:

- **Hook** — immediate curiosity and title potential.
- **Evergreen** — useful after twelve months.
- **Authority** — ability to add informed original analysis.
- **Visual** — suitability for diagrams and faceless production.
- **Commercial** — advertiser relevance without relying on financial advice.
- **Risk** — copyright, factual and policy risk; higher is safer.

## Planned learning sequence

### Videos 1–3: validate the basic promise

Test whether viewers respond to everyday actions explained through invisible infrastructure.

- V01: instant payment.
- V02: card authorization versus settlement.
- V03: interbank money movement.

### Videos 4–6: validate deeper system curiosity

Test whether the audience follows the channel beyond familiar consumer actions.

- V04: Brazilian banking network.
- V05: international financial messaging.
- V06: correspondent and cross-border chain.

### Videos 7–10: validate technical depth and future-facing themes

- V07: addressing and identity.
- V08: liquidity and settlement risk.
- V09: data-sharing infrastructure.
- V10: tokenized value and stablecoins.

## Experiment metrics

Metrics are evaluated after 7 and 28 days for each video.

### Packaging

- impressions;
- click-through rate;
- traffic-source mix;
- title/thumbnail test notes.

### Content

- average view duration;
- average percentage viewed;
- retention at 30 seconds;
- largest retention drop;
- returning viewers;
- comments that reveal unanswered questions.

### Operational

- research time;
- script time;
- asset-production time;
- edit time;
- total monetary cost;
- copyright claims or restrictions.

## Decision thresholds

After V03:

- Continue unchanged if at least one video shows strong packaging and retention relative to the other two.
- Iterate packaging if retention is acceptable but CTR is consistently weak.
- Rewrite format if CTR is acceptable but retention collapses in the first 30 seconds.
- Pause if production exceeds five hours per week for three consecutive videos.

After V10:

- Continue if the channel demonstrates improving retention, repeatable production and at least two topics with meaningful organic discovery.
- Pivot the format or sub-pillar if results concentrate in only one topic family.
- Stop the experiment if production remains expensive, policy risk is high or there is no evidence of audience pull.
