## Sound
- One low-volume royalty-free ambient track
- Subtle UI confirmation sounds created or licensed for reuse
- No recognizable notification sound from a bank or payment application

## Asset rules
- Every asset must be original, public-domain, or licensed for commercial YouTube use.
- Store source URL, license type, author and retrieval date for every external item.
- Do not reproduce a real banking interface.
- Do not display real CPF, phone number, account number or transaction data.
- AI-generated visuals must be reviewed for invented text, logos and misleading system behavior.

## Thumbnail production brief
Primary hypothesis: `O PIX POR DENTRO`

Composition:
- phone with a generic Pix confirmation on left;
- glowing five-layer transaction path on right;
- large text using no more than four words;
- strong contrast at mobile size;
- no Banco Central or bank logo;
- no fake promise such as "segredo revelado".

## Completion gate
Asset production is complete only when all twelve assets have an owner, provenance record, editable source and exported 1920x1080 version.
