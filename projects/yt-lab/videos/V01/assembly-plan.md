# V01 — Assembly Plan

## Target
- Runtime: 7:30–8:30
- Resolution: 1920x1080
- Frame rate: 30 fps
- Loudness target: approximately -14 LUFS integrated
- Peak ceiling: -1 dBTP
- Captions: Portuguese, manually reviewed

## Low-cost toolchain
1. Narration: ElevenLabs free tier when available, or a locally generated neutral PT-BR voice.
2. Diagrams and screens: Canva Free, Figma free tier, or original SVG assets.
3. Editing: CapCut Desktop or DaVinci Resolve Free.
4. Music and sound: YouTube Audio Library or another license documented in the asset ledger.
5. Captions: editor auto-caption followed by complete manual correction.

## Assembly sequence

### Pass 1 — Voice master
- Generate narration in sections of 30–60 seconds.
- Remove unnatural breaths, duplicated syllables and pronunciation errors.
- Confirm pronunciation of DICT, SPI, Conta PI and Pix.
- Join sections into one clean WAV master.

### Pass 2 — Radio edit
- Place narration on timeline.
- Remove avoidable pauses.
- Mark every conceptual transition.
- Confirm runtime before producing detailed visuals.

### Pass 3 — Visual backbone
- Place A01–A12 according to the asset manifest.
- Change visual state every 4–8 seconds.
- Use on-screen text only for names, numbers and the five-step recap.
- Avoid decorative footage unrelated to the explanation.

### Pass 4 — Motion and emphasis
- Animate the direction of messages and settlement.
- Keep debit and credit visually distinct through position and labels, not color alone.
- Use zoom only to clarify hierarchy.
- Never imply that the user's money travels through DICT.

### Pass 5 — Sound and captions
- Add low-volume background music.
- Duck music under narration.
- Add restrained UI sounds.
- Generate and manually correct captions.

### Pass 6 — Quality review
- Watch once without sound: diagrams must still be understandable.
- Listen once without image: narration must still form a complete explanation.
- Watch at 1x on a phone-sized preview.
- Check every external asset against the rights ledger.
- Verify every factual statement against the evidence pack.

## Publication-readiness gate
The video may be presented for human approval only when:
- narration has no obvious synthetic artifacts;
- all visuals are original or have documented commercial-use rights;
- DICT is never described as moving money;
- SPI settlement and Conta PI are represented accurately;
- the video contains meaningful original explanation rather than generic stock footage;
- thumbnail is legible at small size;
- title does not overpromise;
- captions are reviewed;
- final export has been watched end to end;
- publication remains a manual decision.
