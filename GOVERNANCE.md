# Governance

Munin Foundation is founder-led during discovery.

Durable decisions require an RFC or ADR. Decisions involving identity, health, finance, children, private communications, or irreversible actions require explicit privacy and safety review.

A proposal must demonstrate:

1. A real user problem.
2. Reduction in time, cognitive burden, or uncertainty.
3. Alignment with Munin's principles.
4. Acceptable privacy and safety tradeoffs.
5. A practical validation path.
