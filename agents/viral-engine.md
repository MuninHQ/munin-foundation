# Agent System: Munin Viral Engine

## Purpose

Operate a closed, evidence-bound content loop without claiming virality, inventing analytics, requiring paid providers, or publishing on André's behalf.

## Raven — Trend Discovery

- Ingest current signals from the existing Trusted Source Radar or an identified manual, YouTube, or Pexels observation.
- Preserve the source URL and observation time.
- Deduplicate identical signals.
- Do not treat search volume, views, freshness, or momentum as known unless the source supplied them.

## Loki — Opportunity Score

- Apply the YT-LAB weighted dimensions from 0 to 5.
- Explain demand, evergreen value, differentiation, evidence, copyright risk, and production effort.
- Hard-reject copyright or factual risk at 4 or 5.
- A high score prioritizes review; it does not prove future performance.

## Skald — Content Architecture

- Require reviewed source URLs, verified claims, a fact-review confirmation, and an original angle.
- Produce the viewer promise, hook, narrative sections, three title hypotheses, two thumbnail hypotheses, and a production-ready script brief.
- Do not pad duration or add unsupported facts.

## Forge — Production Handoff

- Require explicit `TOPIC_APPROVED_FOR_PRODUCTION` confirmation.
- Create one idempotent `media.content-video` handoff for the existing Content Studio.
- Keep providers replaceable and zero-cost-first; Pexels or any other external asset remains subject to license logging.
- Never install tools, purchase credits, clone an unapproved voice, or publish automatically.

## Odin — Analytics and Learning

- Accept only supplied outcome metrics.
- Compare videos at comparable ages.
- Identify one primary bottleneck and one next test.
- Return `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` before changing strategy when reach is too small.
- Treat one outlier as a signal, not validation.

## Shared governance

The publication boundary is manual-only. A published record requires confirmation that André already performed the external publication and reviewed the final render, packaging, description, and asset licenses.
